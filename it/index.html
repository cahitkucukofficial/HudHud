Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy applies to all apps developed and published by Hudhud Apps (Ahmet Rauf Küçük): “Cash Book CK”, Income Expense Hudhud, “Fuel Pulse” and “Calculator Hudhud”.

1. General principles

  • Our apps require no sign-up or login; you never create a user account.
  • The personal and financial information you enter is never sent to our servers; we cannot see, collect or process it.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools in our apps.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data with any third party.

2. Details by app

Cash Book CK

  • Records: Transactions, accounts, categories, notes and settings are stored only in your device's local storage.
  • Receipt photos: The camera or photo library is accessed only when you choose to attach a photo, with your permission. Photos are stored on your device.
  • Exchange rates: Rates are fetched from the public file of the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye (tcmb.gov.tr) only when you tap the update button; no records or personal information are sent with this request. You can also enter rates manually.
  • File backup & export: Backup, Excel and PDF files are created on your device; you decide where they go.
  • Reminder: The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device; no server is involved.
  • Passcode: Stored on your device, never sent to us.
  • Pro purchases: Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us.

Income Expense Hudhud

All records are stored in your device's local storage and are never sent to a server. Your location, contacts or microphone are not accessed; when you add a photo, your device's own photo/camera picker opens.

Fuel Pulse

“Fuel Pulse” asks for no personal data. Price and news data are downloaded as public files from GitHub Pages, and the national average price from ucuzyakitbul.com.tr. Like any internet request, these servers may see technical connection details (e.g. IP address); we have no access to them and keep no usage logs. Your chosen province/district, favorites and theme are stored on your device.

Calculator Hudhud

“Calculator Hudhud” asks for no personal data and does not use the internet to work. Your calculation history, the calculator’s state and your preferences are stored only in your device’s local storage and are never sent to a server. When you copy a result it is written to your device’s clipboard; the clipboard is never read. Location, contacts, camera and microphone are not accessed. The website link in the app opens in your browser only if you tap it.

My Roots

My Roots needs no account and does not use the internet for your family tree. People, family links, dates, notes, photos, documents, voice recordings and preferences are stored only in your device’s local storage and are not sent to a server. Your photo library or microphone is accessed only when you tap the relevant button to add a photo or recording. Backup files and exports are saved or sent only where you choose.

3. Storage and deletion

If you delete an app, its records on the device are deleted too. For step-by-step instructions see Data Deletion.

4. Children's privacy

Our apps are for a general audience and do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Changes

We may update this policy as we add features; for material changes we update the date on this page.

6. Website: cookies and local storage

  • This website (hudhudapps.com) uses no cookies and contains no ads. To show the total number of visits it uses a cookie-free visit counter (GoatCounter) that does not store personal identities; page-view information is sent to that service.
  • If you choose “Accept”, your selected language and light/dark mode are stored in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) on this device only; nothing is sent to any server.
  • If you choose “No, thanks”, these preferences are not stored and the site opens in your browser’s language on each visit. Only your decision itself (accept/decline) is remembered.
  • You can change your decision at any time with the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer, or remove everything by clearing your browser data.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; as the hosting provider, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy.
  • In “Manage cookies” in the notice you can switch the storing of preferences and the visit counter on or off separately.

For privacy questions: destek@hudhudapps.com

Support

How can we help?

Ran into a problem or have an idea? Email us — mentioning the app and device you use helps us respond faster.

destek@hudhudapps.com

Cash Book CK

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Create a backup file in Settings → Backup and restore it on the new device.

Can I restore an entry I deleted?

Yes. Restore it from Settings → Deleted Records.

What's included in Pro?

Yearly and Summary reports, Excel/PDF export, budget limits for every category, unlimited quick access and all personalization options.

Why is my card balance split into statement and next month?

Based on the card's statement and due dates, spending before the statement date goes on this statement and later spending on next month's.

Income Expense Hudhud

When will Income Expense Hudhud be released?

Soon on the App Store and Google Play; we'll announce the date on this site.

Fuel Pulse

Where do prices come from and how often are they updated?

Province and district prices are updated once a day from public data; news every few hours. Prices are for information only; the price at the station may differ.

Is the app paid?

No. “Fuel Pulse” is free and has no ads.

Calculator Hudhud

Is the app paid?

No. Calculator Hudhud is free and has no ads.

How do the VAT keys work?

+VAT adds the chosen rate to an amount; −VAT removes the VAT from an amount that includes it. You can change the rate under Menu → Style in the Calculator settings section.

How do I delete my calculation history?

Under Menu → History, delete entries one by one with Edit, or all at once with Clear history.

My Roots

Where are my records stored?

Your family tree, photos and voice recordings are stored only on your device and are never sent to any server.

What happens to my records if I change phones?

Under Settings → Backup you can create a backup file and restore it on your new device with Import JSON. Without a backup, records do not move to a new device on their own.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. In Settings you can turn on the app lock and set a PIN.

How do I share or print my tree?

Under Settings → Export you can save or share it as a tree image (PNG), PDF or Excel.

How do I change the app's language?

Under Settings → Language; Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish and Italian are supported.

Data Deletion

Delete your data whenever you want.

Our apps have no user accounts and your records are not kept on our servers. Deletion is entirely in your hands — there's no need to request it from us.

Cash Book CK

  • All records: Settings → Backup → Reset All.
  • A single entry: Press and hold it, or open it and tap Delete. Deleted entries can also be removed permanently from Settings → Deleted Records.
  • Everything: Removing the app from your device deletes all data on the device.

Income Expense Hudhud

Delete your records from the app's settings, or remove the app to clear all data.

Fuel Pulse

The app stores no personal data. Your favorites and preferences live on your device and are removed with the app.

Calculator Hudhud

Under Menu → History you can delete entries one by one with Edit, or delete all of them with Clear history. Your settings live on your device; removing the app deletes all data.
