📖 Contents

🔓 Unlocking

Pick a file or folder (File/Folder button or drag and drop); the locking processes are listed. “Unlock All” closes them all, “Close Selected” only the ticked ones.

🗑️ Force delete

Deletes the locked item; if the lock can't be released it schedules deletion for the next restart.

✏️ Move / rename

Moves or renames a file in use, offering to close the locking process first if needed.

🖱️ Right-click menu

Tick “Add to right-click menu” during setup and Explorer shows “Unlock with Hudhud Unlocker”.

🌐 Language

Choose Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish or Italian from the list at the top right.

💳 Purchase / Registration

The first 30 days are a free trial. Buy a license via Help → “Buy...” and unlock the registered version with your code.

FAQ

The locking program is not listed.

Some system processes are protected and can't be closed. For an item that can't be unlocked, “Delete” tries deletion at the next restart.

Do I lose data when closing a process?

Unsaved data can be lost. Save your work in that program before closing it.

Does the app go online?

Only a small request to check for new versions; “Buy” opens the payment page in your browser. Your file information is never sent.

System requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit); ending processes needs administrator approval.
